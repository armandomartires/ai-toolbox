## Goal

Its guard expects a corpus of numbered tasks, but since `S017.T006` (2026-11-13) this repository's briefs are `tasks/S###.T###_Name.md`, which the generator detects as `sprint_brief`. Make the published dashboard's expected shape match the layout.
