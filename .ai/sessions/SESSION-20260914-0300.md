- Date: 2026-09-13
- Agent/model: opencode (anthropic/claude-opus-5)
- Objective: answer whether the `project-workflow` skill has a handover
  mechanism passing state from one task to the next, and if not, plan one
  — for the skill and for this repo.

- Context consulted:
  - The `project-workflow` skill in full: `SKILL.md`, all four top-level
    templates, all six `reference/` files, and the three `0000_TEMPLATE`

  - This repo's `.ai/`: all five templates, `README.md`, `SPRINT-CURRENT.md`,

- Findings:
  - **No handover contract exists in the skill.** Its task template has
    Goal, Plan, Files touched, Verification, Status notes; nothing names
    inputs or what the next task inherits. The word "session" appears only
    in the size-budget sense.
  - **This repo is ahead of the skill it owns.** `.ai/templates/TASK.md`
    already has `Minimal context`/`Preconditions`/`Dependencies`/
    `Expected result`, and `.ai/sessions/` is a working narrative bridge —
    ~60% of the contract, unnamed, never propagated back.
  - **`00.CONVENTIONS.md` is 3087 bytes against its own ≲3 KB cap.**
    Measured, not estimated. The budget had never been checked.
  - Three further governance-layer defects, all found by reading:
    Phase 4's roadmap header said "in progress" after every criterion was
    met; TASK-0019 was done but unticked in `TODO.md`; B-004's table row
    had fallen outside its table since TASK-0013.

- Decisions (human, this session — recorded in ADR-0012):
  1. **Resumability is the mandatory invariant; one-task-one-session is
     the default, not a rule.** The source research proposed codifying the
     hard rule; rejected against `INDEX.md:15`, where
     `SESSION-20260914-0100` closed five tasks, three ADRs and a review —
     the session that emptied the backlog.
  2. **The validation check ships with the `≥ 0020` numeric boundary**,
     labelled in its own source as omission detection, not contract
     verification.
  3. **Skill first, then this repo adopts deliberately** —
     copy-never-symlink means improving the skill changes nothing here.

- Corrections made to the research proposal, before planning from it:
  - "Add `## Inputs` and `## Outputs`" would have created two owners per
    fact in this repo, where four sections already carry that information.
    Restructured as a **merge**, so section count does not grow.
  - "Machine-checkable contract" was overclaimed. A check sees a heading
    and whether anything is under it; it cannot see whether the declared
    inputs are the real ones. Scoped and labelled accordingly.
