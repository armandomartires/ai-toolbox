# Ad-Hoc Tasks

Things noticed during other work that are real, verified, and out of scope for whatever
was being done at the time; formerly `planning/BACKLOG.md`, whose `B-NNN` rows keep their
number here as `### NNN.` (ADR-0066). An entry is promoted to a `tasks/S###.T###_Name.md`
brief when picked up, and moves to *Resolved* when closed.

**Closing an entry.** Move it under *Resolved*; its *Ready when* line reads
*Closed <date> by `S###.T###`* (or an `ADR-` or review id). That artifact must exist, and a
task must read `done` or `cancelled`; an old `TASK-NNNN` resolves through `.ai/id-map.tsv`.
Update the sentence below in the same commit. At most three open entries are titled
*Unexercised:* (ADR-0039). `scripts/check-backlog-closures.py` checks all of it (B-038, B-047).

**Eleven items are open**, counted from the entries under *Open*. A resolved entry's *Ready when* names its closer (rule: `.ai/00.CONVENTIONS.md`); the full history of each entry is in git (ADR-0033).
